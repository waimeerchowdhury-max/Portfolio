THE APOSTROPHE | LOGO KIT

Faithfully traced from the last approved generated image, with solid colors replacing raster texture. All lettering is outlined: no fonts required. SVG/PDF/EPS are genuine vector paths, not embedded images. Text is editable as shapes, not live type.

SVG: master for Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma and web.
PDF: scalable artwork for print and layout. EPS: legacy print workflows.
Transparent PNG: 6000px wide full logos; 2048px icons for overlays and watermarks.
White/ivory: dark backgrounds. Black: light backgrounds.
Square PNG/JPG: presentations and social posts. Avatar: 1080px icon. ICO: browser favicon.

Palette: ivory #F7F5EF; near black #080808; pure black and white variants included.
Print files use RGB. Ask your printer for conversion to their required CMYK/spot-color profile; no universal CMYK conversion is assumed. Vector artwork has no resolution limit. Embroidery and very small engraving may need vendor-specific simplification.
Keep proportions locked; retain clear space. At tiny sizes use the icon rather than the full logo and small PHOTOGRAPHY line.
