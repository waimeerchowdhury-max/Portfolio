’01 — services

what we shoot

The Apostrophe Media is a photography studio in New York City. Waimeer Chowdhury and Umair photograph natural portraits, studio sessions, events, and street work for people across the city — unposed frames that still feel like the moment they came from.

’01

Portrait sessions

Personal, graduation, couples, and creative portraits shot on location around New York or against a clean studio backdrop. Natural direction, no stiff posing.

  • on-location or in-studio
  • one to two hour sessions
  • edited client gallery
’02

Studio sessions

Controlled lighting with a mood, palette, and wardrobe plan built with you. The same approach behind The Studio Sessions and Spider-Verse chapters on this site.

  • concept and moodboard first
  • colour, shadow, and texture tests
  • editorial-style selects
’03

Event coverage

Birthdays, engagements, cultural nights, and small celebrations across New York City. Documentary coverage that keeps the room looking like the room.

  • candid, low-interruption shooting
  • evening and low-light work
  • group and detail frames

What is The Apostrophe Media?

The Apostrophe Media is a New York City photography studio and working portfolio run by photographer Waimeer Chowdhury, who shoots with creative partner Umair. It covers natural portraits, studio sessions, events, and street photography, and publishes that work as seven online chapters.

Who is the photographer behind The Apostrophe Media?

Waimeer Chowdhury is the founder and photographer. He describes himself as an independent photographer based in New York, and much of the portrait work on the site is made together with his partner Umair.

Where is The Apostrophe Media based?

In New York City. Sessions are booked across the city and the surrounding metro area, and travel further out is arranged case by case.

What kinds of photography do you take on?

Personal portraits, studio shoots, event coverage, and creative or street-led projects. Portrait and studio sessions usually start from a mood, a theme, or a place the client has in mind.

How do I book a session or get a quote?

Email contact@theapostrophe.media or message @theapostrophemedia on Instagram. Every booking starts with a short conversation about ideas, frames, and emotion so the session has a clear target before the camera comes out.

Can I see the full portfolio before booking?

Yes. The site holds seven series and 34 frames in total, plus a page for each of the five people most often in front of the lens. Every series can be opened and played as a full reel from the series section.
